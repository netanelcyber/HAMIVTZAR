Every email should include:

□ Subject line with [CRITICAL ZERODAY] or [URGENT CVE]
□ Product name and version (FortiOS 8.0.0)
□ CVSS score and severity level
□ Number of affected organizations/systems
□ Authentication requirements (None/Yes/Partial)
□ Proof-of-concept availability (Yes/Available in testing)
□ Exploitation difficulty (Moderate/Low/High)
□ Affected sectors (Healthcare, Finance, Government, etc.)
□ Your name and contact information
□ Expected response timeline (24-48 hours)
□ Confidentiality/embargo marking (Coordinated Disclosure)
□ Attached technical documentation
